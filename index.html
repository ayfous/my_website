Hello! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, working with Prof. Ozan Candogan.

I am broadly interested in the intersection of deep learning and combinatorial optimization. My current work focuses on designing deep learning architectures that are both expressive and tractable to optimize over discrete inputs. This is especially important in predict-then-optimize settings, where the learned model is used in a downstream combinatorial optimization problem. I am also developing methods that use deep learning to enhance combinatorial optimization solvers.

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at Columbia University, where I was advised by Prof. Vineet Goyal. My doctoral work focused on developing approximation algorithms for sequential decision-making under uncertainty in supply chains and matching markets.

Prior to my Ph.D., I earned a Master’s and a Bachelor’s degree in applied mathematics from École Polytechnique in France.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.
